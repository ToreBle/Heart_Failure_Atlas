# 07 – LLM workflow and experimental validations

Scripts and outputs for the analysis of the LLM-based workflow as well as the in vivo data from experimental validation of ZLN005.

## Scripts

- **`<pubmed_pathway_workflow_V2>.txt`** – LLM workflow instruction for compound search based on pathways and a reasearch context.
- **`<pubmed_pathway_analysis_2026-08-05_agonist>.json`** – Output of the LLM-based workflow for the cardiomyocyte downregulated pathways in the research context of agonist.
- **`<pubmed_pathway_analysis_2026-08-05_inhibitor>.json`** – Output of the LLM-based workflow for the cardiomyocyte downregulated pathways in the research context of inhibitor.
- **`<Plotting_LLM_workflow_results>.R`** – Visualizations of the output files from the LLM-based workflow.
- **`<Comparision_LLM_workflow_DGIdb>.R`** – Evaluation of the LLM-based workflow and DGIdb for compounds targeting genes of pathways downregulated in cardiomyocytes.
- - **`<Plots_in_vivo_validations>.R`** – Quantification of experimental validations from in vivo data.

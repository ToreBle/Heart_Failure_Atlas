# 05 – BulkRNA-seq and pathway analysis

Scripts for analyis of pathways and bulkRNA-seq complementing the human heart failure atlas.

## Scripts

- **`<STAR_alignment_bulkRNA>.sh`** – bulkRNA-sequencing aligning with STAR.
- **`<BulkRNA_DESeq2>.R`** – Differential gene expression analysis of bulkRNA-seq data.
- **`<ClusterProfiler_Pathway_Analysis>.R`** – Pathway analysis of bulkRNA-seq with ClusterProfiler.
- **`<Pathway_analysis_Cardiomyocytes>.R`** - Pathway analysis of snRNA-seq cell type cluster.
- **`<Bisque_bulkRNA_Decomposition>.R`** - bulkRNA-seq decomposition for fibroblast subcluster.
- **`Ion_channel_expression_cardiac_niches>.ipynb`** - Expression of ion channels in spatial cardiac niches.
